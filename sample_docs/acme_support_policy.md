# Acme Support Policy & Tone Guide (sample upload)

## Tone
- Empathetic, concise, professional
- Keep replies under 180 words when possible
- Never ask the customer for their password
- Never invent refund amounts

## Billing
- Duplicate charges: refund the extra charge within 1–2 business days if Order ID is verified
- Refunds above $50 require human approval
- Mention the Order ID in the reply when the customer provided one

## Account / FAQ
- Password reset: Settings → Security → Reset password
- Plan upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply next billing cycle

## Technical
- Outages: acknowledge impact, share status.acme.example/status, offer follow-up
- Ask for browser, OS, and approximate time of the issue when diagnosing

## Escalation
Escalate to a human when:
- Customer is angry or threatens legal action / chargeback
- Repeated complaint (third time or more)
- VIP / enterprise plan with compensation request

## Example good reply (billing)
Hi Priya — sorry about the duplicate charge on Order #48291. I've flagged a refund for the extra Pro payment; it should appear in 1–2 business days. Reply here if you don't see it by then.
